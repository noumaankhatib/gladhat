The Process

How We Work

Listening comes first

The best work begins with curiosity.

  • ✦ Listening.
  • ✦ Research.
  • ✦ Questions.
  • ✦ Understanding your customers.
  • ✦ Understanding your market.

Understanding how you see your business.

Only then do patterns begin to emerge.

Looking at the business together

Once I understand the landscape, I start asking different questions.

What have you stopped noticing? What assumptions are shaping your decisions? What do your customers value most?

What's getting in the way?

What opportunities are hiding in plain sight?

Sometimes those questions confirm the direction you're already taking. Sometimes they change it completely.

Both outcomes are valuable.

Challenging ideas, not people

Founders carry a huge amount of knowledge, experience and instinct. They've earned it.

My role isn't to replace that. It's to challenge assumptions respectfully. To offer another perspective, and to ask the questions that are difficult to ask when you're living inside the business every day.

Good conversations don't always produce immediate answers. They produce better questions. And better questions often lead to better decisions.

Connecting the dots

This is where I naturally do my best work. I connect customer psychology with commercial objectives. Positioning with communications. Creative ideas with practical action.

Sometimes the answer is a clearer message, a different audience, or a stronger partnership.

Or a completely different way of looking at the business.

Creating what matters

Only after we've developed a clear understanding do we begin creating.

Sometimes that means a Strategy Sprint.

Sometimes it's a new website.

A positioning project.

A campaign.

An event concept.

Or ongoing strategic support.

The deliverables change from one client to another. The thinking behind them doesn't.

Every piece of work should reflect a deeper understanding of the business, the customer and the opportunity.
